## Marking & Grading System (p.32)

The Inspection Checklist is divided into **five major sections**:
1. Design & Facilities
2. Control of Operation
3. Maintenance & Sanitation
4. Personal Hygiene
5. Training & Complaint Handling

Each requirement is marked as **Compliance (C)**, **Non-Compliance (NC)**, **Partial Compliance (PC)**, or **Not Applicable / Not Observed (NA)**. Every requirement is worth **2 marks**, except asterisk-marked (*) requirements, which are worth **4 marks** and are *critical to food safety* — non-observance of any asterisk item automatically results in Non-Compliance (NC cannot be "partial" for these items).

| Finding | Marks (normal item) | Marks (asterisk-marked item) |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance (C) | 2 | 4 |
| Non-Compliance (NC) | 0 | 0 |
| Partial Compliance (PC) | 1 | 0 (not applicable to asterisk items) |
| Not Applicable / Not Observed (NA) | 2 | 4 |

**Definitions:**
- **Compliance (C)** — Requirement fully documented and implemented; no major or minor non-conformance observed.
- **Non-Compliance (NC)** — Requirement not documented and not implemented; major non-adherence observed.
- **Partial Compliance (PC)** — Requirement partially implemented but not documented, or partially documented but not implemented; a lapse that, in the auditor's judgment, is unlikely to cause system-wide failure. *Not applicable to asterisk (*) items.*
- **Not Applicable / Not Observed (NA)** — Requirement does not apply to this FBO and cannot be observed.

**Grading bands** (same across all 8 checklist types):

| Grade | Status | Marks Required |
|---|---|---|
| A+ | Compliance – Exemplar | 90% & above |
| A | Compliance – Satisfactory | 80% – 89% |
| B | Needs Improvement | 50% – 79% |
| No grade | Non-Compliance | Below 50% |

## Notes for implementation

- **Item 25 in Milk Processing**: the source lists this as asterisk-marked but scores it 2 points, inconsistent with every other asterisk item (always 4 pts) — confirm against the real PDF or FSSAI's official download before encoding it, rather than guessing.
- **Item 46 in Milk Processing**: jumps from 43 straight to 46 in the source table with no 44/45 — preserved exactly as printed, since "fixing" it would make your extraction diverge from the real government document the company may cross-check against.
- Each checklist's blank "Comments" field and the C/PC/NC/NA selector column are part of the original form but are *inputs an auditor fills per audit*, not static checklist content — your data structure should separate the two: a static part per item (`item_id`, `question`, `section`, `weight`, `asterisk_critical`) built from this extraction, and a dynamic part per audit run (`status`, `comments`) your application writes.
- Only pages 32–47 (General Manufacturing, Milk Processing, Meat Processing) are included here per your page range (32–50); Slaughterhouse, Catering, Retail, Transport, and Storage & Warehouse (pages 48–69) weren't requested this time — say the word if you want those extracted the same way.
