# Item GM_17: Incoming material, semi or final products are stored according to their temperature and humidity requirement, in a hygienic environment. FIFO & FEFO is practised.

## Requirement Description
Incoming material, semi or final products are stored according to their temperature and humidity requirement, in a hygienic environment. FIFO & FEFO is practised.
