# Item Trans_10: If a vehicle is changed from transporting non-food products to transporting food products, proper cleaning is conducted to avoid contamination of food. Check for records.

- **Category**: [Transport](./index.md)
- **Section**: [Control of Operation](./control_of_operation.md)
- **Weight**: 2 points

## Requirement Description
If a vehicle is changed from transporting non-food products to transporting food products, proper cleaning is conducted to avoid contamination of food. Check for records.
