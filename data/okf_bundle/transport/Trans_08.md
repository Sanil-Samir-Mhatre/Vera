# Item Trans_08: Transport of chilled products is done at 5°C or below and of frozen products at -18°C or below. Check for records or temperature logs.

- **Category**: [Transport](./index.md)

## Requirement Description
Transport of chilled products is done at 5°C or below and of frozen products at -18°C or below. Check for records or temperature logs.
