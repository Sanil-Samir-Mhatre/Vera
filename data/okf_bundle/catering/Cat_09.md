# Item Cat_09: Equipment and containers are made of non-toxic, impervious, non-corrosive material which is easy to clean & disinfect.

- **Category**: [Catering](./index.md)
- **Section**: [Design & Facilities](./design_facilities.md)
- **Weight**: 2 points
- **Critical (Asterisk)**: No

## Requirement Description
Equipment and containers are made of non-toxic, impervious, non-corrosive material which is easy to clean & disinfect.
