# Item Cat_22: Frozen food is thawed hygienically. No thawed food stored for later use (meat/fish/poultry in fridge <=5°C or microwave; seafood cold running water <=15°C within 90m).

- **Category**: [Catering](./index.md)
- **Section**: [Control of Operation](./control_of_operation.md)
- **Weight**: 4 points
- **Critical (Asterisk)**: Yes — Non-Compliance cannot be partial

## Requirement Description
Frozen food is thawed hygienically. No thawed food stored for later use (meat/fish/poultry in fridge <=5°C or microwave; seafood cold running water <=15°C within 90m).
