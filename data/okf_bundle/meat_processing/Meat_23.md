# Item Meat_23: Room used for deboning, preparing, packing or handling of meat is equipped with adequate facilities for cleaning & disinfecting implements (knives, cleavers, saws) not used for other purposes.

- **Category**: [Meat Processing](./index.md)
- **Section**: [Control of Operation](./control_of_operation.md)
- **Weight**: 4 points

## Requirement Description
Room used for deboning, preparing, packing or handling of meat is equipped with adequate facilities for cleaning & disinfecting implements (knives, cleavers, saws) not used for other purposes.
