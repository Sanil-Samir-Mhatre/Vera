# Item SW_26: No person suffering from a disease or illness or with open wounds or burns is involved in handling of food or materials which come in contact with food.

- **Category**: [Storage & Warehouse](./index.md)
- **Section**: [Personal Hygiene](./personal_hygiene.md)
- **Weight**: 2 points
- **Critical (Asterisk)**: No

## Requirement Description
No person suffering from a disease or illness or with open wounds or burns is involved in handling of food or materials which come in contact with food.
