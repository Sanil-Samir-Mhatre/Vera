# Item MP_20: Raw milk when brought to dairy plant by farmers, should reach the plant within 4 hours from milking & is cooled to 5°C or lower as quickly as possible.

- **Category**: [Milk Processing](./index.md)
- **Section**: [Control of Operation](./control_of_operation.md)
- **Weight**: 2 points
- **Critical (Asterisk)**: No

## Requirement Description
Raw milk when brought to dairy plant by farmers, should reach the plant within 4 hours from milking & is cooled to 5°C or lower as quickly as possible.
