# Item MP_08: Equipment and containers are made of non-toxic, impervious, non-corrosive material which is easy to clean & disinfect (preferably SS 316 for equipment & SS 304 for tanks/tankers).

- **Category**: [Milk Processing](./index.md)
- **Section**: [Design & Facilities](./design_facilities.md)
- **Weight**: 2 points
- **Critical (Asterisk)**: No

## Requirement Description
Equipment and containers are made of non-toxic, impervious, non-corrosive material which is easy to clean & disinfect (preferably SS 316 for equipment & SS 304 for tanks/tankers).
