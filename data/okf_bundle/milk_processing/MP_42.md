# Item MP_42: No person suffering from a disease or illness or with open wounds or burns is involved in handling of food or materials which come in contact with food.

- **Weight**: 2 points

## Requirement Description
No person suffering from a disease or illness or with open wounds or burns is involved in handling of food or materials which come in contact with food.
