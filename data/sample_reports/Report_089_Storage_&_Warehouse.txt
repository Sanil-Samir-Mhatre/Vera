On 2026-06-25, food safety auditor Siddharth Varma conducted an on-site audit at Shivalik Agro Industries situated in Dharwad, Karnataka (Tier-3). Critical non-compliance observed in frozen products are stored in frozen rooms maintained at -18°c or below: the facility failed to implement required controls and lacked active verification records. Regarding food business has an effective customer complaints redressal mechanism, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records.
