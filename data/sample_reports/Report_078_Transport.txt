Facility: Vindhya Cold Chain Logistics
Auditor: Preeti Deshmukh | Date: 2026-05-26

On 2026-05-26, food safety auditor Preeti Deshmukh conducted an on-site audit at Vindhya Cold Chain Logistics situated in Hyderabad, Telangana (Metro). Requirement for the interior of vehicle is kept clean and there are no seepage, cobwebs, dirt, dust, etc is not applicable to current facility operations and scope. Regarding bulk foodstuffs in liquid, granulate or powder form are transported in containers/tankers reserved for transport of foodstuffs & marked "for foodstuffs only", the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the design & construction of transport vehicle permit adequate maintenance, cleaning, storage of food and do not provide entry or harbourage to pests, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding in case of breakdown of vehicle or cooling unit, an alternative vehicle conforming to standards is provided promptly to transport foods to ensure safety and hygiene, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding food handlers have the necessary knowledge and skills & trained to handle food safely, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding food handlers maintain personal cleanliness and personal behaviour, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding if a vehicle is changed from transporting non-food products to transporting food products, proper cleaning is conducted to avoid contamination of food, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding transport of chilled products is done at 5°c or below and of frozen products at -18°c or below, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the containers/crates used for transporting food are made of food grade material and designed to prevent contamination, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding temperature monitoring devices are calibrated at a pre-determined interval, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Observation for annual medical examination & inoculation of food handlers against the enteric group of diseases as per recommended schedule of vaccine is done: partial implementation was noted, though formal documentation or log frequency showed procedural gaps. Regarding foods which become unfit during transportation whether due to temperature abuse or otherwise are identified and disposed off in a manner precluding human consumption, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding transporter has an updated fssai license, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding mix loads of food material are adequately compartmentalized to prevent cross contamination, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Observation for food handlers equipped with suitable aprons, gloves, headgear, shoe cover etc; wherever necessary: partial implementation was noted, though formal documentation or log frequency showed procedural gaps. Regarding vehicle is inspected for debris, dust, previous loads, pest activity & other non-food or hazardous item before loading, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding no person suffering from a disease or illness or with open wounds or burns is involved in handling of food or materials which come in contact with food, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Requirement for pest control program is available & pest control activities are carried out by trained and experienced personnel is not applicable to current facility operations and scope. Regarding transport vehicle is equipped to achieve & maintain the required temperature, wherever required and designed in a way that allows monitoring the temperature from outside, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Requirement for appropriate documentation & records are available and retained for specific period is not applicable to current facility operations and scope. Regarding hazardous material like chemicals, fuels, flammable materials are not transported with food material, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the vehicle has proper locking/sealing facility to ensure that products are not exposed to external weather conditions and pilferage, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the vehicle is covered and inside structure is intact, well painted with no signs of rusting, flaking paint & corrosion, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records.
