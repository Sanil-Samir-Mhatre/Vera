Category: Slaughterhouse
Facility: Malabar Cold Chain Logistics
Auditor: Dr. Rajeshwar Sharma | Date: 2026-05-21
Profile: Clean

On 2026-05-21, food safety auditor Dr. Rajeshwar Sharma conducted an on-site audit at Malabar Cold Chain Logistics situated in Cuttack, Odisha (Tier-2). Regarding preventive maintenance of stunning equipment is carried out regularly per manufacturer instructions, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding when dressed meat has to stored without further immediate processing, storage is maintained at 0°c to 2°c, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding annual medical examination & inoculation of food handlers against enteric group of diseases per recommended vaccine schedule is done, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding food business has an effective consumer complaints redressal mechanism, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding knives and sharpeners (mushtala) are of stainless steel and sterilized/sanitized before use, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding all animals are subjected to ante-mortem examination and post-mortem inspection by authorised veterinarian as per specified proforma, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding an equipped laboratory is available with qualified chemist/analyst & veterinary microbiologist, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding food handlers maintain personal cleanliness (clean clothes, trimmed nails & waterproof bandage) and behaviour (hand washing, no loose jewellery/smoking/spitting), the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding blood, manure, garbage, hide, viscera and offals removed within 8 hours after slaughtering without contamination; containers cleaned/disinfected immediately, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding no signs of pest activity or infestation in premises (eggs, larvae, faeces etc), the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding equipment used for stunning is maintained in good working condition, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the equipment & containers (except chopping blocks, cutting boards & brooms) are durable, made of corrosion resistant material facilitating cleaning, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding internal structure & fittings are made of non-toxic, impermeable materials which prevent entry of dirt, dust & pest, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the layout of slaughter house ensures forward movement from live animal introduction to emergence of meat and offals without overlap/intersection, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding adequate ventilation is provided within the premises, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding humane methods are used for slaughtering, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding appropriate documentation & records are available and retained for 1 year or shelf-life of product, whichever is more, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding walls are paved with impervious glazed tiles upto 1m for small ruminants/poultry and upto 5m for large animals & epoxy coated, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the dressing of carcasses is not done on floor, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding sufficient space for separation and storage of condemned carcasses, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding food handlers have necessary knowledge and skills & trained to handle food safely, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding clean potable cold water (meeting standards of is 10500) with pressure hose pipes and supply of hot water is available in slaughter hall, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding food establishment has an updated fssai license & noc from local authority, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Requirement for premises have sufficient lighting i is not applicable to current facility operations and scope. Regarding there is an adequate resting area (lairage) with facility for watering and examination of animals or birds, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Observation for food handlers equipped with suitable aprons, gloves, headgear, shoe cover etc: partial implementation was noted, though formal documentation or log frequency showed procedural gaps. Regarding internal / external audit of system is done periodically, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding doors are smooth, non-absorbent surface, close fitted & self closing (where appropriate), the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding pest control programme is available and pest control activities are carried out by trained and experienced personnel, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding floors are impervious, epoxy coated and non-slippery, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding no person suffering from illness or open wounds/burns involved in handling food or food-contact materials, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding carcasses/organs fit for consumption marked as inspected & passed; unfit marked as inspected & condemned, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding slaughter house refuse and waste processed into animal by-product or dumped in covered pits, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding personnel hygiene facilities available including toilets, hand washing with warm/hot and cold water, hygienic drying adjacent to toilets and processing entry, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding efficient drainage system; drains/gutters fitted with traps and screens, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding for large slaughter house, etp is required, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding cleaning of equipment, food premises is done per schedule & programme, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding animals suspected of infectious diseases are segregated and kept in separation, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding the premises have separate provision for the slaughter of different species of animals & for different methods of slaughter, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records. Regarding windows & other openings are free from accumulated dirt, those which open are fitted with insect-proof screen, the facility demonstrated full compliance and verified all related operational records.
